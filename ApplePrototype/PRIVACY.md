# Solidcraft for iPad Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Solidcraft for iPad is provided by Christopher Wallace. The app does not collect personal data or transmit your designs to the developer. Its CAD engine and resources are bundled with the app and run on your device.

Designs you save are stored on your device in the app's storage. When you import or export a file, you choose the file or destination using the system file interface. Files saved to a cloud provider are subject to that provider's privacy practices. The app may also store diagnostic information locally in its Documents folder.

Apple may provide services such as App Store distribution and may handle information under its own privacy policy. If you contact us for support, information you choose to provide will be used to respond to your request.

For questions or support, visit [the Solidcraft repository](https://github.com/cwallyman/solidcraft).
